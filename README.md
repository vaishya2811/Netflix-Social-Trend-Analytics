# 🎬 Netflix Social Trend Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Netflix has thousands of movies and TV shows available across different countries, genres, and audience categories. Analyzing such large volumes of content manually can be challenging and time-consuming.

This project leverages **Python, SQL, Power BI, Git, and GitHub** to transform raw Netflix and IMDb datasets into actionable insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, business intelligence queries, and interactive dashboards.

The goal is to uncover content trends, audience preferences, platform growth patterns, and movie performance metrics that can support data-driven decision-making in the entertainment industry.

---

## 🚀 Business Problem

Streaming platforms generate massive amounts of content-related data every day. Understanding:

* Which genres dominate the platform
* Which countries contribute the most content
* How content libraries grow over time
* What type of content audiences prefer
* How highly-rated movies perform

is critical for strategic planning and content investment decisions.

This project demonstrates how data analytics can convert raw entertainment data into meaningful business insights.

## 📈 Key Insights

### Netflix Dataset

* Movies significantly outnumber TV Shows.
* The United States and India dominate content production.
* TV-MA is the most common content rating.
* Netflix experienced rapid content growth after 2015.

### IMDb Dataset

* Highly rated movies are not always the most voted movies.
* Drama remains one of the most dominant genres.
* Audience engagement varies significantly across movie categories.
* Ratings and votes together provide a deeper view of movie performance.
